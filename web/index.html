      Strategy Methodology & Technical Framework

      Transparent, deterministic scoring architecture without unexplainable black-box models

      1. The 5-Pillar Explainable Scoring Model

      Bearabull evaluates all 50 constituent equities of the NIFTY 50 index at the conclusion of every trading session. The weekly signal engine synthesizes 5 orthogonal quantitative dimensions into a single directional score (0–100):

      35% Weight

      Trend Regime

      Evaluates long-term, intermediate, and short-term moving average structure:

      • Price vs 200-day Simple Moving Average (SMA 200)
      • Golden alignment: Price > SMA 50 and SMA 20 > SMA 50
      • Short-term momentum: 9-day EMA vs 21-day EMA
      • Supertrend (10-period, 3.0 ATR multiplier) directional regime
      • Average Directional Index (ADX 14) and +DI / -DI directional bias
      25% Weight

      Momentum Dynamics

      Measures price velocity and cycle inflection points:

      • MACD (12, 26, 9) Line, Signal line crossover, and Histogram expansion
      • RSI 14 Wilder's smoothing: 50–70 healthy bull momentum; <35 oversold; >75 overbought consolidation
      • Stochastic Oscillator (%K 14, %D 3) crossovers
      15% Weight

      Volume Confirmation

      Validates institutional participation behind price moves:

      • Relative Volume (RVOL): Today's volume vs 20-day average (>= 1.25x confirmation)
      • 14-day Rate of Change (ROC)
      • On-Balance Volume (OBV) trend confirmation
      15% Weight

      Price Action & Breakouts

      Identifies structural chart inflection points:

      • 20-day Swing High and Swing Low breaks
      • Volume-supported breakout and breakdown detection
      • Unfilled opening gap-up and gap-down momentum
      10% Weight

      Volatility & Risk Filter

      Protects capital by sizing risk during unstable regimes:

      • Average True Range (ATR 14) for dynamic stops
      • Bollinger Bands (20, 2) expansion vs squeeze
      • 20-day annualized historical volatility ceiling (>35% flags risk)

      2. Signal Decision Thresholds

      BUY
      Bullish Score >= 70, Bullish Score exceeds Bearish by at least 10 points, and acceptable data quality. Suggested entry is near support/close with 1.5x ATR stop loss.
      SELL
      Bearish Score >= 70, Bearish Score exceeds Bullish by at least 10 points. Indicates strong technical breakdown or negative directional bias.
      HOLD
      Consolidation Regime: Neither directional threshold is met. Reflects rangebound price action, mixed indicators, or pending breakout confirmation.
      INSUFFICIENT DATA
      Fewer than 50 historical daily candles available, or missing crucial market fields. Signals are withheld to prevent deceptive outputs.
